Our spot: Turn 3–4 hill

Find the hill on the map above and check how far it is from Lot F before Sunday. Chairs and blanket go down the moment the gates open, and one of us always stays on the spot.

Sunday game plan

Most fans arrive around 9–10 am, so being at COTA before the gates open is how we get a good spot. The times below are targets, not promises.

  1. 7:15 am be at COTA in the Lot F queue. Allow 60–90 minutes for the drive on race morning.
  2. 7:30 am parking lots open
  3. 8:00 am at the gate, chairs and blanket in hand
  4. 8:30 am gates open. Walk straight to the Turn 3–4 hill.

Leaving after the race: Lot F has one exit lane, and waits of an hour or two are normal. Powder Room at 8 pm is tight, so we may ask to move it back.

Parking

Lot F, pre-purchased by Patne for all days. You don't need to buy anything. The pass is for the car, so we all go in together.

Lots open 8:00 am Friday, 7:30 am Saturday and Sunday. COTA says every car needs a pre-bought pass to get onto COTA Blvd. Route from Austin: TX-130 S, exit 451 to Elroy Rd, then right on COTA Blvd and follow the Lot F signs.
