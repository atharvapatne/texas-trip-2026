Our spot

Turn 3–4 GA lawn. It sits at the north end of the circuit, near Turn 1. Pick a parking lot near that end so the walk is short, and scout the lawn on Friday and Saturday: a slope to sit on, a screen in view, a restroom and water within five minutes.

Water refill points are at Turn 1, Turn 3, the Main Grandstand and Grand Plaza.

Allowed

  • Collapsible chairs and blankets
  • One empty water bottle, 32 oz or less
  • Small hand-held umbrellas
  • Sunscreen in a plastic bottle
  • Bags up to 12 × 12 × 20 in
  • Empty hydration packs

Not allowed

  • Food and drinks, alcohol included
  • Coolers and thermoses
  • Aerosol sunscreen, glass
