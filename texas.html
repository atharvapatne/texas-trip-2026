Saturday home-cooked lunch

Lunch at 1:00 pm at home for all five, right after Ambika arrives, and out the door for COTA by 1:45 pm. Prep starts at 9:45 am so the pot is simmering before Chaitrali leaves for the bus stop at 11:30.

Menu is open. An easy fit is one big pot (biryani or pulao with raita) plus a separate egg dish, since Hetvi and Anu do not eat eggs, so nobody cooks while the clock runs. Vote below.
